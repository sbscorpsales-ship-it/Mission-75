# GATIVX Brand System

## Approved palette
- Orange: #FF6A00 — primary energy/action colour
- Midnight navy: #0A1A4F — trust, depth and the wordmark X
- White: #FFFFFF — clarity and contrast
- Light orange: #FF8A1A — gradient highlight
- Deep orange: #E84400 — gradient finish

## Logo hierarchy
1. Master identity: white GX runner/arrow emblem; Gativ in white; X in midnight navy; full acronym; “A Param IT Product™”; BELGAUM.
2. App icon: GX emblem only, no small text.
3. Dark variant: midnight navy ground with white GX and orange runner/arrow accent.
4. Light variant: white ground with orange/navy brand elements.

## Acronym
GOAL | ACTION | TRACK | IMPROVE | VITALITY | eXCEL

## Product UI
- Dark mode: midnight navy surfaces, white text, orange primary actions.
- Light mode: white surfaces, midnight navy text, orange primary actions.
- Keep all existing navigation, member data, health provenance and app behaviour intact when applying visual styling.

## Usage
Use the app icon at small sizes. Use the complete master logo only where supporting copy remains readable. Preserve square 1:1 proportions for app icons and do not stretch logo artwork.
